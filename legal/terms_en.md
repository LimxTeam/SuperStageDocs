## 3. Product Positioning, Current Scope, and Boundaries

### 3.1 Product positioning

SuperStage is a stage-lighting and performance-visualization plugin built on Unreal Engine. Its main uses are: stage-lighting previsualization in virtual scenes; DMX workflow testing (Art-Net, sACN); fixture patching and checking (Patch, Fixture ID, Universe, Address); MVR/GDTF and grandMA2/grandMA3 project data preparation and exchange; media and NDI preview; and fixture-library and stage-asset placement.

Specific features, supported Unreal Engine versions, operating systems, and plan differences are as stated in your order, the release notes for your version, and the product documentation.

### 3.2 Current plans and modules

As of the Publication Date, the plan and module structure is as follows (the pricing page and your order prevail):

- **Plans (editions):** Free Trial, Pro, Ultra, Team, and Education;
- **Core stage tools:** Pro includes the SuperStage core module: lighting, pixel matrices and strips, DMX laser patterns, stage effects, fountains, machinery previsualization, procedural scenery, crowds, screens and projection, patching, MVR/GDTF, grandMA preparation and DMX recording. Using existing fixtures and importing GDTF are distinct from hand-authoring fixtures as described below;
- **Enhanced modules:** Ultra adds **SuperLaser** (laser animation creation, SVG / ILDA workflows, external point-cloud input and recording / playback), **SuperNdi** (live NDI video input and recording / playback) and **FixtureBuilder** (fixture authoring without code). Team additionally provides agreed SuperTailor C++ custom-development rights. Trial and Education modules and permitted uses are as stated on the pricing page and order;
- **Every edition supports 512 DMX input universes**, with 512 channels per universe, over Art-Net and sACN. Achievable scene scale and refresh performance depend on the project, network and hardware;
- **SuperShader (stage-effect shaders) is a free module** requiring no paid license;
- Concurrent-use rules, commercial-use rights, and terms per plan are set out in Sections 4 and 5 and on the pricing page.

This is a description of the current structure, not a promise that every feature is available in every version, plan, or scenario.

### 3.3 Not a live-site safety system

SuperStage's previsualization, simulation, patching, data exchange, media preview, DMX input/output, and network interactions are aids for design, checking, communication, and testing. Software output may differ from real-world results due to hardware, network, fixture firmware, third-party software, format interpretation, system load, or site conditions.

**You must not use SuperStage as the sole safety interlock, emergency stop, personnel protection, fire protection, mechanical limit, laser safety, pyrotechnic safety, drone flight safety, electrical protection, or any other life-safety system.**
